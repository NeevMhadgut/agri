Irrigation Advisory Workflow

Step-by-step process of telemetry acquisition and AI decision formulation:

  1. Collect farm profile and sugarcane crop stage details.
  2. Read telemetry from IoT soil moisture sensors and micro-weather stations.
  3. Analyze soil moisture deficit against crop evapotranspiration coefficients.
  4. Generate plot-specific irrigation dosage and run-time recommendations.
  5. Share actionable advisory notifications with farmers and field officers.

Data Sources

  • Soil Moisture Sensors (Capacitive FDR probes at 15cm and 30cm)
  • Weather Forecast (Temperature, humidity, wind velocity, and rainfall probability)
  • Satellite Images (Sentinel-2 NDVI vegetation health indicators)
  • Crop Information (Sugarcane variety, phenological growth stage, and root depth)
  • Soil Reports (Texture, organic carbon percentage, and field water retention capacity)
  • IoT Devices (LoRaWAN gateways, automated solenoid valves, and pressure transducers)

Stakeholders and Responsibilities

  • Farmer
    • Checks daily crop water needs on the web portal.
    • Follows precise drip irrigation duration advice.
  • Agricultural Officer
    • Reviews field sensor calibration and telemetry reports.
    • Confirms and approves automated advisory alerts before dissemination.
  • Researcher
    • Studies seasonal sugarcane evapotranspiration and aquifer depletion patterns.
    • Improves and retrains advisory decision algorithms.
  • Sugar Factory Management
    • Monitors regional crop maturity and cane harvest scheduling.
    • Supports large-scale canal and community drip irrigation logistics.

Agricultural & Technical Abbreviations

AI
Artificial Intelligence — Computational models simulating agricultural decision-making.
IoT
Internet of Things — Network of physical sensor nodes collecting telemetry in real-time.
GIS
Geographic Information System — Framework for capturing and analyzing spatial farm data.
GPS
Global Positioning System — Satellite navigation system providing exact plot boundary geofences.
ET
Evapotranspiration — Sum of evaporation from soil and transpiration from sugarcane crop leaves.
